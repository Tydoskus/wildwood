# Gravebloom

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool. The original transparent PNG is used at `public/assets/wildstat/gravebloom-boss-v1.png`. The renderer adds breathing, attack motion, and damage flashes; root and spore telegraphs are drawn separately from shared gameplay geometry.

## Generation prompt

Create production game sprite artwork for Gravebloom, the boss of Verdant Catacombs in a colorful top-down fantasy RPG. One 1024x1024 square PNG with a truly transparent background and clean alpha. Single full-body imposing guardian made of pale ancient bone, tangled woody roots, layered deep green moss, and glowing mint/cyan mushrooms. Broad readable silhouette, massive root forearms, a skull-like stone mask with luminous teal eyes, an antler crown of roots with small mushrooms, a glowing flower-shaped core in its chest. Short powerful rooted legs. Stylized hand-painted 3D game art with crisp edges, strong shapes, softly shaded volumes, consistent 3/4 slightly overhead game camera, facing down and slightly left. Center the creature with all extremities inside canvas, 8% transparent padding. No scenery, no ground plane, no floor, no frame, no text, no labels, no watermark, no rectangular backdrop. Soft light from upper left, pale bone and leafy olive tones with restrained turquoise bioluminescence. This will be displayed at 380px in gameplay, so prioritize readable anatomy and silhouette over fine texture.

## Regular enemies

Six original animated SVG sheets are generated by `node scripts/build-verdant-enemies.mjs`. Each contains idle, walk, and attack frames. Their atlas metadata lives in `src/game/enemy-atlases/verdant-crypt.mjs`.
