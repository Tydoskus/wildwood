    What the clock models: current enemy HP/rewards/spawns and the server’s complete loot catalog, percentage damage, health, and regeneration equipment, 8%-per-level gear upgrades, attack cap, research, loot odds, travel, respawns, critical-hit expected damage for regular enemies and personal bosses, map-specific boss regeneration and respawns, and a repeat-boss farming comparison. Mixed runs give each campaign a slightly different blend of nearby, efficient, DPS-first, and boss-readiness priorities; the boss-farm strategy is an explicit exploit check. Opening momentum, equipment strength, and future-system reserve are reference/what-if controls only. Headroom ceilings are event-clock sensitivity estimates. The clock starts on Forest arrival with completed tutorial rewards. Boss readiness and camp-clear settings are player-strategy assumptions, not gameplay requirements. Black Boots activate after five combat-free seconds. Loot is delivered immediately in this model; server batching is not simulated. The clock deliberately does not invent player dodging, deaths, idle time, boss mechanics, or multiplayer contribution.
